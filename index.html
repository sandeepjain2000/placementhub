Why PlacementHub

PlacementHub goes beyond managing campus placements. We believe placements are an outcome—not the objective. The real objective is to build industry-ready graduates through continuous collaboration.

PlacementHub is SaaS software operated by PlacementHub Solutions. We do not guarantee employment, salaries, or placement outcomes, and we do not handle candidate salary or offer payments.

What we provide

Technology to help institutions, students, and employers manage and coordinate placement activities—not employment guarantees.
