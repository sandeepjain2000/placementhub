College registration and participation

College registration must be initiated by the Training and Placement Officer (TPO) only, and must be completed before student participation is arranged.

  1. 1 Create or activate your college account

    PlacementHub — Campus Placement & Engagement Platform

    1. Click on College Admin.
    2. Solve captcha.
    3. Enter name, email, mobile, college name, city, and state.
    4. Enter password.
    5. Click Create account.
  2. 2 Account authentication

    Please notify the PlacementHub team once these steps have been completed so that the administrator can authenticate your account.

  3. 3 Review the competition

    Once the account is linked, the published competition is shared with the college for review.

  4. 4 Approve for campus

    Log in to the college dashboard, open Internships & Programs, review the listing, and select Approve for campus.

After registration and campus approval, PlacementHub will guide the TPO through the remaining participation steps.
