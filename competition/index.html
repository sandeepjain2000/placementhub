Submission requirements

Each team must submit a presentation that strictly follows the PowerPoint template provided by PlacementHub.

The presentation should cover:

  • Problem and proposed solution
  • Target users
  • Implementation approach
  • Business model and financial viability
  • Potential impact

A video recording is optional.

Competition and evaluation process

The competition follows a two-stage process:

  • Initial screening — Submitted presentations are screened to shortlist teams.
  • Final presentations — Shortlisted teams present their business cases online, followed by a Q&A with industry experts.

Entries are evaluated on originality, problem relevance, practicality, business viability, financial considerations, and potential impact.

College registration and participation

College registration must be initiated by the Training and Placement Officer (TPO) only, and must be completed before student participation is arranged.

  1. Create or activate your college account Coordinate with PlacementHub to open or activate the college account on the PlacementHub portal.
  2. Complete account linking Work with PlacementHub Solutions to finish partnership and account-linking.
  3. Review the competition Once the account is linked, the published competition is shared with the college for review.
  4. Approve for campus Log in to the college dashboard, open Internships & Programs, review the listing, and select Approve for campus.

After registration and campus approval, PlacementHub will guide the TPO through the remaining participation steps.
